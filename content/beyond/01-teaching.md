Forty-seven people, none of whom nod along forever to an explanation that has
not landed. You find out quickly which parts were only ever clear to you.

It is the same failure as a design document nobody argues with because nobody
finished reading it. Explaining a thing until someone else can use it turns out
to be the same skill on both sides of a whiteboard.
